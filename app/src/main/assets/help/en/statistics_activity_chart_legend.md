# What you see

You see a graph of the number of sessions played over the last 30 days (for more details, see the section "[About the trainer](app://info)").
![help_activity_chart]

## Graph elements

- **1** - the number of sessions played over the day, the maximum value of the scale depends on the intensity of training.
- **2** - calendar, the displayed dates correspond to the beginning of the week.
- **3** - the number of sessions played over the day.
- **4** - the number of sessions played today.
