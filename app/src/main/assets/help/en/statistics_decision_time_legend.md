# What you see

You see a graph of the time you spent on making a decision to choose one of the options on average per day over the last 30 days (for more details, see the section "[About the simulator](app://info)").
![help_decision_time_chart]

## Graph elements

- **1** - time scale spent on making one decision on average per day.
- **2** - calendar, the displayed dates correspond to the beginning of the week.
- **3** - your indicators, calculated as the average value for all daily selection attempts.
