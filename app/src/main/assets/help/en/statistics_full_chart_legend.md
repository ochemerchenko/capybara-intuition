# What you see

You see a graph of the daily average success of your sessions over the past month.

the number of sessions played over the past 30 days (for more details, see the section "[About the simulator](app://info)").
![help_full_density_chart]

## Graph elements

- **1** - scale of the average success of sessions over the day in percentage.
- **2** - calendar, the displayed dates correspond to the beginning of the week.
- **3** - baseline, displays the mathematical probability of choosing the desired option. The value of the level of this line is calculated as (100% / level), where level is the number of buttons on the on-screen selection form.
- **4** - area of ​​the mathematically predicted result. Displays the variance, which is calculated over the total number of attempts over the day. If your results are in this area - this is mathematically expected behavior, your results are fully explained by the mathematical theory of probability.
- **5** - your results. To calculate this indicator, the success of a single session is defined as the ratio of the number of guesses to the number of all attempts during the session, and then the average success of sessions during the day is calculated.
