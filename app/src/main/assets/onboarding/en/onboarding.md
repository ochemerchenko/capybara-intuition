# Welcome to Intuition!

Thank you for deciding to try **Intuition**.

This application is created for people who are interested in better understanding themselves in the decision-making process.

## Why Intuition?

We all have to make a lot of decisions every day. Sometimes everything is clear, everything is known, and then the decision becomes the result of a simple calculation.

But sometimes there is not enough data and knowledge to make a decision. Sometimes there is not enough. Sometimes it seems that there is no data at all.

But a decision must be made. And then, unlike artificial intelligence, a person uses Intuition.

## What will it give me?

Modern science fully recognizes intuition, but rejects its mystical explanation. It is believed that a person can process a huge amount of data simultaneously, including non-verbalized data, unconsciously.

Intuition is a simple and understandable tool for exploring your ability to make decisions under conditions of uncertainty.

Perhaps you will be able to make it an effective simulator of your own intuition. It depends on your desire and readiness to listen to yourself, to your own feelings, to observe and name them.

Perhaps you will be able to explore how your self-confidence and your attention to yourself affect your performance.

**Intuition** provides the opportunity to consciously observe yourself and analyze historical data on your success exactly like "intuition".

## How to use it?

Go to the main screen with guessing options. Choose one of the proposed options. Look at the result. Tap anywhere on the screen, make the next choice.

Observe your own feelings before making a choice and after the result is displayed. Try to give these feelings names, this is important.

Explore the statistics accumulated by the application.

Play, train, test - explore yourself. And you will be happy :)

## I wish you an interesting game!

Do not try to beat the application.

Try to understand yourself better.

Let Intuition help you discover new patterns in your own decision-making process.

That is what it was created for.
