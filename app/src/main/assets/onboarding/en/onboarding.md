# Welcome to Intuition!

Thank you for choosing the **Intuition** app.

This application is created for people who are interested in better understanding themselves in the decision-making process.

## Why Intuition?

We all have to make a lot of decisions every day. Sometimes everything is clear and then the decision becomes the result of a simple calculation.

But sometimes, there is not enough data and knowledge to make a decision. And sometimes, it seems like there is no data at all.

But a decision must be made. And then, unlike artificial intelligence, a person uses Intuition.

## What will it give me?

Modern science fully recognizes intuition, but rejects its mystical explanation. It is believed that subconsciously, a human is capable of processing a vast amount of data simultaneously, including non-verbalized information.

Perhaps, you will manage to make this app an effective trainer for your own intuition. It depends on your desire and readiness to listen to yourself, to your own feelings, to observe and name them.

Perhaps you will be able to explore how your self-confidence and self-awareness affect your performance.

**Intuition** provides the opportunity to consciously observe yourself and analyze historical data regarding your success specifically as an "intuition".

## How to use it?

Go to the main screen with options to guess. Choose one of the offered options. Check the result. Tap anywhere on the screen, then make the next choice.

Observe your feelings before making a choice and after the result is revealed. Try to give name to these feelings — this is important.

Explore the statistics accumulated by the app.

Play, train, test - explore yourself. And happiness will follow 🙂

## I wish you an interesting game!

Do not try to beat the app — it is not your opponent.

Try to understand yourself better.

May Intuition help you discover new patterns in your own decision-making process.

That is what it was created for.
