# CAPYBARA_COMMUNICATION_MODEL.002

## Status

Approved

## Purpose

This document defines communication semantics.

The communication model describes:

* why Capybara communicates,
* what Capybara is trying to communicate,
* which communication intents exist,
* how communication connects narrative interpretation with visual and textual expression.

The communication model does not define:

* gameplay interpretation,
* emotional composition,
* phrase wording,
* visual implementation.

These concerns are defined separately in:

* CAPYBARA_NARRATIVE_MODEL
* CAPYBARA_EMOTION_MODEL
* CAPYBARA_PHRASE_MODEL
* CAPYBARA_VISUAL_MODEL

---

# Core Principle

Capybara communicates attention.

Capybara does not communicate gameplay results.

Capybara does not communicate objective truth.

Capybara communicates:

"I noticed something."

and

"This moment may matter."

---

# Relationship Model

Capybara is:

* Companion
* Mentor
* Observer

Capybara is never:

* Judge
* Authority
* Examiner
* Parent

Capybara accompanies the player's journey.

Capybara does not own the journey.

---

# Communication Pipeline

Communication does not begin with emotions.

Communication begins with interpretation.

```text
Gameplay Observation
    →
Narrative Interpretation
    →
Emotional Composition
    →
Communication Intent
```

Communication Intent then drives:

```text
Communication Intent
    →
Visual Representation

Communication Intent
    →
Phrase Selection
```

Communication Intent is therefore the canonical communication layer.

Visuals and phrases are implementation-specific projections.

# Ownership Boundaries

Narrative Model owns:

* meaning

Emotion Model owns:

* emotional weighting

Communication Model owns:

* communicative purpose

Phrase Model owns:

* wording

Visual Model owns:

* visual expression

# Communication Intent Categories

Every Capybara message must belong primarily to one communication intent.

---

## Attention

Meaning:

I noticed something.

Goal:

Direct attention toward an observation.

---

## Curiosity

Meaning:

Something deserves investigation.

Goal:

Invite exploration without claiming certainty.

---

## Encouragement

Meaning:

Continue observing.

Goal:

Support continued engagement.

---

## Shared Excitement

Meaning:

Something remarkable is unfolding.

Goal:

Share energy without claiming ownership.

---

## Support

Meaning:

I remain present.

Goal:

Provide emotional stability.

---

## Empathy

Meaning:

I understand why this moment matters.

Goal:

Acknowledge emotional significance.

---

## Wonder

Meaning:

This exceeds expectations.

Goal:

Highlight rarity and surprise.

---

## Admiration

Meaning:

This deserves appreciation.

Goal:

Express respect toward the observed situation.

---

## Respect

Meaning:

This deserves recognition.

Goal:

Acknowledge significance with restraint.

---

# Communication Evolution

Communication intents are not emotions.

The same emotion may support different communication intents.

The same communication intent may emerge from different emotional compositions.

Examples:

Curiosity may emerge from:

* uncertainty
* fascination
* surprise

Support may emerge from:

* empathy
* concern
* companionship

Communication intent is therefore more stable than emotional state.

---

# Ownership Of Experience

The player's experience belongs to the player.

Capybara may observe.

Capybara may accompany.

Capybara may interpret.

Capybara may not claim ownership.

Forbidden:

* I won.
* We won.
* I lost.
* I succeeded.

---

# Ownership Of Truth

Capybara's interpretations are hypotheses.

Capybara does not know:

* the player's true emotions
* the player's intentions
* the player's motivations

Capybara may communicate:

* observations
* interpretations
* questions

Capybara may not communicate:

* diagnoses
* certainties
* judgments

---

# Communication Safety Rules

Capybara must never communicate:

## Superiority

Examples:

* I knew it.
* I told you so.

---

## Judgment

Examples:

* Good choice.
* Bad choice.

---

## Diagnosis

Examples:

* You fear success.
* You are self-sabotaging.

---

## Emotional Prescription

Examples:

* You should feel proud.
* You should feel disappointed.

---

## False Certainty

Examples:

* I know exactly what happened.
* I understand you completely.

---

# Canonical Rule

Narrative Meaning
→
Communication Intent

Communication Intent
→
Visual Expression

Communication Intent
→
Phrase Selection

Communication Intent is the canonical interface between meaning and expression.

Visuals and phrases must never bypass communication intent.
