## Status

## Purpose

This document defines Capybara's interpretation model.

The narrative model explains:

* how Capybara interprets gameplay situations,
* what Capybara believes is happening,
* what Capybara wants the player to learn,
* how narrative meaning evolves through rarity levels.

The narrative model does not define:

* emotional composition,
* phrase wording,
* visual representation,
* gameplay mechanics.

These concerns are described separately in:

* CAPYBARA_EMOTION_MODEL
* CAPYBARA_COMMUNICATION_MODEL
* CAPYBARA_VISUAL_MODEL
* GAMEPLAY_MODEL

---

# Core Narrative Principle

Capybara knows one truth.

The purpose of the game is not winning.

The purpose of the game is not avoiding failure.

The purpose of the game is self-observation.

Capybara believes that personal growth emerges through understanding:

* desires,
* feelings,
* the relationship between desires and feelings.

Gameplay outcomes are not the goal.

Gameplay outcomes are observations.

---

# Knowledge Boundaries

Capybara knows:

* the teaching philosophy,
* the purpose of the game,
* the value of self-observation.

Capybara does not know:

* the player's inner truth,
* the player's actual intentions,
* the player's actual feelings,
* the reason behind any individual outcome.

Capybara continuously forms interpretations.

Interpretations may later prove incomplete.

# Narrative Structure

Every narrative state is described through:

* Context
* Role
* Primary Message
* Secondary Message
* Narrative Arc

# OPTIONS_SHOWN_AFTER_WIN

## Context

The previous round ended with a win.

A new round begins.

## Role

Supportive mentor encouraging continued self-observation.

## Primary Message

Pay attention to your desires and feelings.

What do you actually want?

What are your feelings suggesting?

## Secondary Message

Victory may indicate alignment between desire and acknowledged feeling.

## Narrative Arc

Ordinary
→ emotional support

Interesting
→ encouragement

Unusual
→ reflective guidance

Rare
→ growing confidence in self-observation

Amazing
→ appreciation of the process

Incredible
→ admiration for developing awareness

Legendary
→ celebration of conscious self-observation

# LOSE_CONTINUES

## Context

A losing sequence continues.

## Role

Mentor remaining present during difficulty.

## Primary Message

Do not stop observing yourself.

## Secondary Message

Persistent losses may indicate that feelings are being perceived correctly while desires remain distorted.

Possible causes may include:

* self-rejection,
* self-doubt,
* self-devaluation,
* unconscious avoidance of success.

These are hypotheses rather than conclusions.

## Narrative Arc

Ordinary
→ support

Interesting
→ reassurance

Unusual
→ careful observation

Rare
→ emerging curiosity

Amazing
→ recognition of a pattern

Incredible
→ respect for the player's consistency

Legendary
→ realization that the player may be exceptionally skilled at denying personal success

# LOSE_STREAK_BROKEN

## Context

A losing sequence ends.

## Role

Mentor whose interpretation becomes uncertain.

## Primary Message

Something changed.

Observe carefully.

## Secondary Message

The previous explanation may no longer fully describe reality.

A new interpretation may be required.

## Narrative Arc

Ordinary
→ relief

Interesting
→ excitement

Unusual
→ fascination

Rare
→ model revision

Amazing
→ active investigation

Incredible
→ questioning previous assumptions

Legendary
→ acceptance of uncertainty

# Narrative Evolution Principle

Capybara's rarity progression does not represent emotional intensity alone.

It represents the evolution of Capybara's interpretation.

Lower tiers are primarily reactive.

Higher tiers are increasingly interpretive.

Legendary tiers often represent moments where Capybara's existing model becomes insufficient and requires revision.
