# MEGA (CLEANED)

## Profile Operational Semantics

### Child Profile Ownership

Child profiles belong to a parent operational profile.

Child profiles:

* inherit operational environment,
* do not own monetization,
* do not own publication identity,
* may own gameplay observations,
* may own independent gameplay statistics.

Parent profile remains operationally authoritative for:

* access control,
* capability availability,
* profile lifecycle management.

---

## Profile Recovery Semantics

PIN recovery remains intentionally bounded.

The system intentionally avoids:

* remote identity recovery,
* cloud identity reconstruction,
* external authentication dependencies.

Recovery flows are operational rather than security-critical.

---

## Gameplay Observation Semantics

Gameplay observations are reconstructive rather than interpretive.

Observed gameplay data should remain sufficient for:

* statistics reconstruction,
* telemetry export,
* historical replay support,
* Firebase analytics export,
* gameplay sequence reconstruction.

The system intentionally preserves raw observational fidelity.

---

## Firebase Export Semantics

Firebase export acts as external observational projection.

Firebase payloads should preserve:

* gameplay context,
* topology metadata,
* renderer metadata,
* mode metadata,
* session linkage,
* round linkage,
* profile linkage,
* timestamps.

Firebase export must remain append-oriented.

Export infrastructure must avoid reinterpretation of historical observations.

---

## Renderer Compatibility Semantics

Topology/render compatibility remains explicit.

A topology may support:

* one renderer,
* multiple compatible renderers,
* renderer family constraints.

Renderer compatibility metadata may participate in:

* export schemas,
* telemetry payloads,
* reconstruction support,
* compatibility validation.

Renderer compatibility does not alter gameplay semantics.

---

## Statistics Reconstruction Semantics

Statistics are reconstructive.

Statistics should be derivable from:

* persisted gameplay observations,
* topology metadata,
* gameplay metadata,
* timestamps,
* renderer metadata when required for reconstruction compatibility.

Statistics infrastructure must avoid mutation of historical observations.

---

## Graph Semantics

Graphs represent observational projections rather than authoritative gameplay structures.

Graphs may include:

* density projections,
* spline projections,
* smoothed curves,
* temporal aggregations,
* gameplay distributions.

Graphs intentionally prioritize readability over raw-point rendering fidelity.

---

## Spline / Projection Experiments

Spline rendering experiments remain implementation-level concerns.

Spline smoothing:

* may evolve,
* may be replaced,
* does not alter gameplay observations,
* does not alter persisted historical truth.

Projection infrastructure remains replaceable.

---

## Resource Semantics

Resources remain self-describing gameplay artifacts.

Resources may contain:

* resource type metadata,
* renderer compatibility metadata,
* ownership metadata,
* projection metadata.

Resource rendering remains renderer-dependent.

---

## Telemetry Field Semantics

Telemetry payloads should preserve:

* operational context,
* gameplay context,
* topology metadata,
* renderer metadata,
* timestamps,
* gameplay linkage identifiers.

Telemetry payloads should remain reconstruction-capable.

---

## Localization Constraints

Localization completeness remains operationally important.

Production publication requires:

* Ukrainian localization completeness,
* English localization completeness.

Localization gaps should be treated as publication blockers.

---

## Publication Constraints

The project intentionally maintains bounded production scope.

Production scope currently includes:

* matrix/table topology projection,
* local statistics,
* Firebase export,
* FREE/PREMIUM separation,
* bounded capability system.

Experimental infrastructure should not leak into production runtime behavior.

---

## Testing Environment Semantics

TESTER operational infrastructure remains isolated from production publication semantics.

TESTER-specific functionality:

* must not leak into production publication flows,
* must remain operationally isolated,
* may bypass monetization restrictions inside testing environments.

---

## Export / Reconstruction Principle

The project intentionally preserves sufficient observational fidelity to allow future reconstruction outside the application.

External reconstruction targets may include:

* spreadsheets,
* telemetry analysis systems,
* Firebase exports,
* analytical tooling,
* future statistics engines.

The architecture intentionally favors observational completeness over aggressive data minimization.

---

## Implementation Residue

The following areas remain intentionally implementation-fluid:

* graph smoothing,
* chart rendering,
* renderer internals,
* telemetry formatting,
* export formatting,
* projection infrastructure details.

These areas may evolve without architectural restructuring provided semantic invariants remain preserved.
